A grounded, collaborative approach

You don’t have to navigate it alone.

We believe meaningful change begins when you feel seen, heard, and accepted. Therapy here is a partnership: warm enough to feel safe, honest enough to help you grow, and practical enough to carry into everyday life.

Whether you’re facing a difficult season or patterns that have followed you for years, we’ll help you understand what’s happening and move toward what matters.

Meet your counselors

Support that understands how differently we grow.

Roger and Marilyn offer specialized online counseling for neurodivergent teens and adults throughout Texas.

  • LPC, LCDC

    Roger Hancock

    Helping teens and young adults navigate life with confidence.

    Roger works with teens and emerging adults as they build self-understanding, strengthen executive functioning, navigate transitions, and prepare for adulthood in ways that honor how they think and learn.

    • ADHD

    • Anxiety
    • Executive functioning
    • School and college transitions
    • Identity and self-confidence
